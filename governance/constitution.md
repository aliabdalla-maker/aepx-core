# AEP-X Constitution v1.0 (Draft)

Per Instructional Manual §1.4 — Month-1 working draft, not the manual's 150–300 page aspiration.

## Preamble

AEP-X exists to be the universal, low-cost, evidence-first interoperability layer for agentic AI — connecting agents, tools, models, and enterprise systems without requiring any of them to trust each other blindly. It will never bypass human authority in financial, legal, healthcare, or child-related decisions (Law 6, [book-of-laws.md](book-of-laws.md)).

## Article I — Purpose

Reduce the cost, risk, and integration effort of deploying agentic AI at enterprise scale, while keeping every decision auditable, explainable, and governed.

## Article II — The Ten Laws

See [book-of-laws.md](book-of-laws.md) — not restated here per Instructional Manual §1.4 ("copy them in verbatim as the seed").

## Article III — Governance Structure

Interim: Founder + advisor. Full Technical Steering Committee targeted Month 18 (ADLC Plan §11).

## Article IV — Standards Lifecycle

Draft → Review → Frozen → Deprecated. No breaking changes within v1.x (SEMVER discipline, ADLC Plan §8).

## Article V — Human Authority

Final human authority for: financial decisions, legal decisions, healthcare decisions, child-related decisions (Law 6) — corresponding to Safety classes S3 (High Risk) and S4 (Critical Risk), where human oversight is mandatory before execution.

## Article VI — Amendment Procedure

Amendments require Technical Steering Committee review once constituted; until then, founder-approved with an explicit rationale recorded in [decisions/](decisions/).
