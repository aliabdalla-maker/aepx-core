**Full description** (max 4000):

AEP-X is the companion app for the AEP-X Agentic IDE, a governed workspace where a crew of AI agents plans your software
changes and a person approves every step.

Use the app to:
• See your projects and agent runs at a glance
• Read each plan before anything happens: requirements, design, proposed changes and review
• Approve or reject a plan with one tap, with a confirmation so nothing is approved by accident
• Approve or deny individual tool calls, each identified by a fingerprint of its exact arguments
• Follow the audit trail of who decided what, and when

Plans
• 30-day free trial with every feature (ad-supported)
• Pro: £4.99 a month for 95% fewer ads. Cancel any time in Google Play.
• Free plan after the trial: keep working, with ads
One account works across the web IDE, desktop apps, the iOS apps and editor plugins.

Built for teams that want AI help without losing control: approval screens never show ads, sign-in uses your
organisation's identity provider, and every decision is recorded.

Requires an AEP-X account (sign in with your organisation).
