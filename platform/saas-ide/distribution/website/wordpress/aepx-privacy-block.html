AEP-X privacy policy

AEP-X Agentic IDE: web, desktop, Android, iOS and editor plugins

Last updated: 2026-11-15. Provider: Equality Software Ltd, London, United Kingdom ("we").

What we collect and why

  • Account: the identifier and profile your organisation's sign-in provider gives us, to sign you in and keep your workspace separate from others.
  • Your work: projects, files, agent plans, approvals and audit records you create, so the service works and every decision is traceable.
  • Subscription: your plan, trial dates and the store's subscription reference (Stripe, Google Play or the App Store). Card details are handled by those stores, never by us.
  • Media Studio (optional): prompts and files you approve for generation or posting are sent to the provider or social network you choose, only after an owner or admin approves each one.

Advertising

The free trial and free plan show ads from Google (AdMob in the mobile apps, AdSense on the web). Google may use device identifiers or cookies to serve and measure ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland we ask for your consent first with Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy options in the app. Without consent, only limited, non-personalised ads are shown. Approval screens never show ads. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the processors needed to run the service (hosting, payment stores, Google for ads, and any media provider you choose), or when the law requires it.

Retention and security

Workspace data is kept while your account is active and deleted on request or when your organisation closes its account, except audit and billing records we must keep by law. Data is encrypted in transit and isolated per organisation.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict processing. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

AEP-X is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 16.
